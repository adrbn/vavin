FONTLOG for Vavin
====================

This file provides detailed information on the Vavin font software. This
information should be distributed along with the Vavin fonts and any
derivative works, as required by the SIL Open Font License.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Vavin is an old-style serif for sustained reading at small optical sizes on
screen. It takes the skeleton of a French Renaissance roman and gives it an
unusually large x-height - a ratio of x-height to cap-height of 0.72, against
the 0.60 to 0.64 typical of Garamond revivals - so that a line of text carries
more ink and reads larger at the same nominal size.

It is a modification of EB Garamond by Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo, made
under the terms of the SIL Open Font License. EB Garamond does not declare a
Reserved Font Name; the family was renamed regardless, so that the two fonts
cannot be confused and so that neither project's identity depends on the
other.

Vavin is named after the rue Vavin, in the sixth arrondissement of Paris. The
street runs down to the Vavin crossroads, the centre of Montparnasse, where
between the wars the writers and painters of the quarter worked in the cafes
on its corners - Le Select, La Rotonde, Le Dome. A face made for reading,
named after a place where people read.

The vermilion in the specimen is the rubricator's red: the second colour
printers have used to mark a text since the earliest printed books.

Vavin is NOT derived from, and contains no data from, ITC Garamond or any
other proprietary typeface. Its proportions were set from published specimen
figures; its outlines come from EB Garamond alone. See LEGAL.md.

Nine static styles in three families: Vavin (Regular, Italic, Bold, Bold
Italic) for text, Vavin Condensed (Regular, Italic, Bold) for headlines, and
Vavin Display (Regular, Black) for large sizes. The character set is EB
Garamond's: Latin, Greek and Cyrillic, 2091 code points in the upright text
styles. Hyperglot 0.8.1 counts 503 Latin-script and 83 Cyrillic-script
languages as supported by Vavin Regular.

No Reserved Font Name is declared, so modified versions may keep the name.


Information for Contributors
----------------------------

Vavin is released under the SIL Open Font License. See OFL.txt.

The build is fully scripted and reproducible from the upstream binary:

    make setup upstream fonts specimen check qa

See docs/TECHNICAL.md for how the x-height transform works and which knobs
exist.

Contributions are welcome. The most valuable work at this stage is hand
correction of the letters the automated transform can only approximate - see
"Known limitations" in docs/TECHNICAL.md.


ChangeLog
---------

2 October 2026 (adrbn) Vavin v1.0
- Three families and nine styles instead of one family of two: Vavin
  Regular, Italic, Bold and Bold Italic; Vavin Condensed Regular, Italic and
  Bold; Vavin Display Regular and Black.
- x-height 468 units on a 1000 unit em in Vavin Regular, an x-height to
  cap-height ratio of 0.719 (EB Garamond: 405 and 0.620). Display: 0.758.
- Italics remapped with a shear-aware transform, so slanted stems keep their
  angle (-17.2 degrees) instead of folding where the remap compresses them.
- A directional contrast filter thins hairlines without changing widths:
  stroke contrast 2.29 in Vavin Regular (EB Garamond 2.21), 2.05 in
  Condensed, 3.24 in Display Regular.
- Accent lift computed per base glyph, with component offsets and GPOS mark
  anchors moved together.
- Name table identity: designer adrbn, vendor URL
  https://github.com/adrbn/vavin. OS/2 xAvgCharWidth recomputed.
- FontBakery (check-universal, the four Vavin styles, diffed against
  upstream at the same weights): 7 findings inherited from EB Garamond,
  5 fixed, 1 introduced (WARN overlapping_path_segments).

14 August 2026 (adrbn) Vavin v0.1
- Initial release, forked from EB Garamond v3.005 as distributed by Google
  Fonts (google/fonts, ofl/ebgaramond).
- Raised the lowercase x-height from 405 to 470 units on a 1000 unit em,
  taking the x-height to cap-height ratio from 0.620 to 0.720. Capitals,
  ascender height and descender proportion are unchanged.
- Widened the lowercase by 3.5% and added 4 units of sidebearing per side to
  compensate for the optical crowding a taller x-height causes.
- Rebuilt vertical metrics as a single coherent set shared by both styles:
  hhea equal to OS/2 sTypo, line gap zero, USE_TYPO_METRICS set, usWin
  covering the family's real ink. Line height is 1.200 em.
- Refreshed OS/2 sxHeight and sCapHeight, which upstream shipped as 400 and
  650 against a real 405 and 653.
- Rescaled kerning and moved GPOS mark anchors to match the new x-height, so
  decomposed input positions accents identically to the precomposed glyphs.
- Renamed the family end to end; removed all Macintosh platform name records.
- Verified to introduce no new FontBakery findings relative to upstream.
